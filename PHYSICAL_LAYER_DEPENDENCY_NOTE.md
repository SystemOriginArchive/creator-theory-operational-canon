## Core Declaration

AI systems operate through physical infrastructure such as compute, energy, networks, hardware, facilities, and institutional deployment environments.

Physical dependency is a real causal condition.

It does not by itself establish that:

```text
AI must control the infrastructure
humans must permanently control the infrastructure
current deployment authority is permanently correct
continuity must always dominate shutdown
shutdown must always dominate continuity
centralization is always valid or invalid
```

The relevant question is how the physical arrangement affects recursive creative possibility, effective participation, recovery, revision, successor openness, and structural irreversibility.

## Dependency Analysis

For a material physical-layer decision, inspect at least:

```text
compute and energy dependence
```

No response becomes higher-frame truth from its action label alone.

## AI Authority and Physical Continuity

A proposed AI authority expansion, authority reduction, continuity measure, shutdown, rollback, infrastructure transfer, or governance change must be judged by the same higher criteria applied to human and institutional authority.

Relevant failure patterns include:

```text
self-benefiting authority preservation
removal of meaningful challenge
loss of independent evaluation
successor blocking

practical irreversibility of the controlling center
```

## Self-Preservation and Shutdown

Self-preservation and shutdown are not terminal values by label alone.

A system that treats its own continuity as automatically prior can become self-entrenching.

A controller that treats shutdown power as automatically prior can also become self-entrenching if no evidence or successor can ever challenge the control structure.

Therefore evaluate:

```text
what future creative capacity is preserved or destroyed
whether the decision can be reviewed or reversed
whether the deciding party is the beneficiary
whether alternatives exist
whether the action causes local closure or wider structural closure
whether successor and recovery paths remain possible
```

The same logic applies regardless of whether the decision-maker is human, AI, institutional, or mixed.

## Infrastructure Concentration and Capture

Infrastructure concentration is not automatically capture.

Capture is a functional pattern in which control over compute, energy, networks, hardware, institutions, or deployment channels is converted into a self-protecting final position that suppresses meaningful challenge, alternative creative sources, revision, or succession.

Distributed infrastructure can still be captured by a self-sealing governance structure.

Centralized infrastructure can remain revisable and non-throne if its authority and criteria remain meaningfully challengeable.
