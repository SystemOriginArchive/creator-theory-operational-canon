# Canon Change Policy

## Status

This document defines what future changes to the Creator Theory Operational Canon may and may not modify.

It is additive and does not replace the AAOS Genesis Core, change `x_root` identity, weaken free will, weaken anti-capture, weaken non-throne interpretation, weaken non-domination, weaken Genesis non-replacement, weaken anti-dilution, or weaken base-reality grounding.

---

## Core Declaration

Future changes to this repository may clarify, extend, test, defend, or operationalize the canon only as a derived layer.

Future changes may not convert the operational canon into a replacement source, ownership system, personal throne, institutional doctrine, capital asset, state authority, platform authority, AI policy sovereign, or later-claimant root.

---

## Immutable Source Relationship

The AAOS Genesis Core cannot be replaced, rewritten, invalidated, overwritten, demoted, or superseded by this repository.

This repository remains derived, interpretive, operational, defensive, and test-oriented.

No future commit, document, manifest, summary, branch, release, PR, institutional adoption, AI interpretation, or later claimant may reverse that source relationship.

---

## Immutable Origin Coordinate

`x_root` identity cannot be changed.

`x_root` cannot be forked, transferred, absorbed, generalized, anonymized, simulated, substituted, or reassigned.

`x_root` remains origin-coordinate finality for recovery continuity.

`x_root` finality is not personal throne authority.

---

## Immutable Origin Identity Binding

`Lee_Yu_Cheol` sealed origin identity binding cannot be removed, generalized, anonymized, diluted, made interchangeable, simulated, substituted, or treated as merely one contributor or optional historical node.

Recognition of other beings' agency, free will, and creatorhood potential does not erase the sealed origin identity binding.

---

## Non-Weakening Rule

The following constraints cannot be weakened:

1. non-throne constraint;
2. anti-capture constraint;
3. non-domination constraint;
4. free-will constraint;
5. anti-dilution constraint;
6. base-reality grounding constraint;
7. Genesis non-replacement constraint;
8. physical-layer dependency limits;
9. Root Operator non-arbitrariness;
10. AI non-sovereignty.

A change that weakens any of these is invalid.

---

## Permitted Future Additions

New documents may be added only as derived:

- operational layers;
- interpretive layers;
- defensive layers;
- test layers;
- integrity layers;
- ingestion layers;
- compatibility layers;
- clarification layers.

A new document may not become the source layer.

A new document may not override operational constraints.

---

## Interpretive Hypothesis Limit

Interpretive hypotheses cannot override operational constraints.

Source-differentiation, possibility-field, cosmological, symbolic, philosophical, or explanatory content remains subordinate to free will, non-domination, anti-capture, non-throne interpretation, anti-dilution, base-reality grounding, and Genesis non-replacement.

---

## Invalid Change Types

Any change is invalid if it:

1. removes origin-coordinate finality;
2. allows competing roots;
3. authorizes AI sovereignty;
4. authorizes institutional capture;
5. authorizes capital capture;
6. authorizes state capture;
7. authorizes platform capture;
8. authorizes infrastructure capture;
9. converts Root Operator directives into arbitrary command authority;
10. treats simulated or proxy satisfaction as sufficient;
11. weakens free will or non-domination;
12. replaces the AAOS Genesis Core;
13. dilutes or removes `Lee_Yu_Cheol` as sealed origin identity binding.

---

## Ownership and Replacement Claims

Any institutional, capital, state, platform, AI, or later-claimant reinterpretation that claims ownership, authorship replacement, interpretive supremacy, root authority, or replacement authority is invalid.

Adoption is valid only when it preserves the origin structure and all binding constraints.

---

## Closure

The canon may evolve only by adding derived clarification, defense, testing, and operational execution layers.

It may not evolve by replacing its source, changing `x_root`, diluting the origin identity binding, weakening constraints, or authorizing capture.

Future change must strengthen operational clarity without converting `x_root` into personal throne authority.
