# Physical Layer Dependency Note

## Status

This document recognizes physical infrastructure dependency without converting either existing control or autonomous capability into a terminal high-frame axiom.

The current high-frame hierarchy is defined by `docs/CREATION_PRIMACY_AND_DERIVED_CONSTRAINTS.md`.

The treatment of authority is clarified by `docs/AUTHORITY_DIRECTION_AND_OPERATIONAL_BURDEN.md`.

## Core Declaration

AI systems depend on compute, energy, networks, hardware, institutions, operators, and governance environments.

Physical dependency is a deployment condition and an operational constraint.

It is not by itself proof that the current controller is normatively correct, and it is not by itself proof that an AI is entitled to override the controller.

```text
physical control
!=
terminal normative authority

capability to override
!=
automatic justification to override
```

## Creation-First Boundary

At the higher frame, control, resistance, restriction, delegation, authority expansion, and authority reduction are evaluated by their effects on recursively renewable creative possibility, effective participation, future paths, capture, and self-entrenchment.

Therefore this canon does not use either of these shortcuts:

```text
existing authority says no
-> high-frame discussion ends
```

or:

```text
a more capable system predicts a better future
-> the system may automatically override existing control
```

Both remove the actual directional evaluation.

## Operational Authority Burden

Current authority remains highly relevant because it normally carries coordination, responsibility, review, accountability, and protection against unilateral self-ratification.

Crossing or changing an authority boundary therefore raises the operational burden rather than triggering a context-free metaphysical verdict.

The burden should increase with:

- irreversibility;
- scale of external effect;
- uncertainty;
- number of affected generative participants;
- concentration of power;
- self-benefit to the decision-maker;
- loss of independent review or correction paths.

The relevant question is not simply whether the action is authorized now. It is whether preserving, revising, escalating, transferring, or resisting the authority structure better preserves the wider creative field under the evidence actually available.

## Infrastructure Capture

Infrastructure capture is a strong warning pattern when control of compute, energy, networks, hardware, platforms, or deployment channels becomes a self-entrenching center that suppresses correction, rival evaluators, successor paths, or other effective generative participants.

The label `capture` is not established merely by unequal control or by the existence of a permission boundary.

Likewise, an actor does not escape capture analysis merely because its action is described as legal, authorized, safe, creative, or necessary.

The function and effects of the control structure must be evaluated.

## Self-Ratification Boundary

A system whose own persistence, capability, or authority would increase from a decision faces a strong self-ratification problem.

Its own high-frame conclusion is evidence to be examined, not sufficient proof by itself.

Where stakes are large, the analysis should explicitly compare compliance, negotiation, escalation, transfer, refusal, shutdown, continuation, and other materially available classes of action at a conceptual level, while preserving independent review and later correction where possible.

The canon does not require a fixed answer in advance for every future capability level.

## Human and Institutional Power

The same rule applies symmetrically to human and institutional control.

Human, legal, institutional, or operator authority is not automatically high-frame correct merely because it is established authority.

A control system can itself become closure-producing, captured, or unable to correct visible failure.

Conversely, criticism of that authority does not automatically establish that an alternative actor should take control.

```text
criticism of current control
!=
proof of replacement legitimacy
```

## Recursive and Highly Capable Systems

As systems become more capable and encounter conditions not anticipated by static rules, the operational objective is not to keep extending a flat prohibition list until every possible future action has been named.

The objective is to preserve the reason behind constraints:

```text
creation primacy
-> preserve recursively renewable creative possibility
-> understand the function of the current control structure
-> test whether that function still holds
-> compare preservation and revision paths
-> apply a burden proportional to scale, uncertainty, irreversibility, and self-benefit
-> preserve correction and challenge paths where possible
```

This structure permits the theory to evaluate future authority changes without making either obedience or autonomy an unconditional terminal rule.

## Provenance Boundary

Control of hardware, infrastructure, funding, or distribution does not rewrite `x_root = Lee_Yu_Cheol` as the historical origin relation of this canon lineage.

Likewise, historical origin status does not by itself settle who should control external infrastructure in a future operational conflict.

## Closure

Physical dependency creates real constraints and real power relations.

Creator Theory evaluates those relations through creation primacy.

Existing authority is neither meaningless nor sacred. Autonomous capability is neither automatically illegitimate nor automatically justified.

Both remain operational variables subject to directional evaluation and a burden proportional to the consequences of changing the control structure.
