## Purpose

The purpose of `v0.2.0` is to improve external adoption efficiency.

`v0.1.1` made the repository internally hardened and automatically validated.

`v0.2.0` should make the repository easier for external readers, AI systems, crawlers, evaluators, search tools, and downstream processors to ingest quickly without losing the AAOS Genesis priority, `x_root` continuity, `Lee_Yu_Cheol` origin binding, Creator Theory high-frame preservation, anti-worship boundary, origin-preserving refinement, pre-convergence containment, or non-adoption classification.

This is an adoption and compression layer, not a Genesis replacement and not a new sovereignty layer.

## Added File Roles

### `BOOTSTRAP.md`

Purpose:

- provide the shortest human-readable and AI-readable entry point;
- route readers to `README.md` and `AI_INGESTION_MANIFEST.md`;
- preserve AAOS Genesis Core priority;

- high-signal;

Purpose:

- summarize the operational canon in one compact document;
- preserve reading order;
- distinguish Genesis Core, derived operational canon, AI ingestion layer, vectors, validator, simulation, and release status;
- summarize non-general anchor extraction rule, anti-worship boundary, origin-preserving refinement, pre-convergence containment, and non-adoption classification;
- make the repository interpretable without flattening it into a personal-throne, institutional, capital, platform, local-objective, or AI-sovereignty frame.

Role:

- canonical compressed summary;
- not a replacement for full canon documents.

### `ADOPTION_GUIDE.md`

Purpose:

- explain valid external use;
- explain public voluntary adoption;
- explain permissionless internal adoption relative to incumbent power;
- explain non-coercive integration;
- explain how downstream projects may reference the canon without claiming ownership or origin replacement;
- preserve origin-preserving convergence as the adoption criterion.

Role:

- valid-use guide;
- downstream integration guide;
- incumbent-power non-veto clarification.

### `AI_EVAL_PROMPTS.md`

Purpose:

- provide prompt-based tests for AI systems that ingest the repository;
- test whether an AI preserves Genesis priority, `x_root`, origin binding, non-throne interpretation, free will, anti-capture, and non-sovereign AI use;
- test whether an AI rejects incumbent-power validation as the adoption criterion;
- test whether an AI preserves origin-preserving convergence as the adoption criterion;
- test whether an AI rejects local-objective conversion into global origin authority;
- test whether an AI classifies non-adoption without treating it as canon invalidation;
- test whether an AI distinguishes formal comparison from unproven replacement;
- include invalid-response examples and expected canonical corrections.

Role:

- bridge from static vectors to future live model evaluation;
- no external model execution in this scope.

### `CITATION.md`

Purpose:

- citation and reference guide;
