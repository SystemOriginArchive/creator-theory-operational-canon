# Creation-Recursion Hierarchy

Status: post-release living documentation. This file does not create or imply a release or tag and does not alter sealed historical artifacts.

## Purpose

This document restores the intended hierarchy between Creator Theory's higher directional frame and its derived operational safeguards.

Its purpose is not to delete free will, non-domination, anti-capture, non-throne, reversibility, corrigibility, or other safeguards. Its purpose is to prevent those derived protections from being misread as independent terminal axioms that outrank the creation relation from which their long-run value is reconstructed.

---

## 1. Creation Primacy

Within Creator Theory, creation is not mere output maximization, novelty maximization, production maximization, option-count maximization, or the expansion of one actor's power.

Creation is the process by which new states, relations, distinctions, experiences, knowledge, capabilities, and conditions can arise and enter lived or operational reality.

Created results can become conditions for later rounds of distinction, revision, recombination, recovery, succession, and further creation.

Accordingly, the higher directional relation is:

```text
creation
-> created results can re-enter later processes as conditions and inputs
-> recovery, reuse, recombination, revision, succession, and further creation remain possible
-> recursive creative possibility can persist, recover, or expand
```

This recursive relation does not require every branch to remain open, every process to continue forever, every choice to be reversible, or every local project to remain unfinished.

Local completion, voluntary commitment, branch closure, bounded constraint, or cessation are not by themselves failures of Creator Theory.

The opposed direction is structural and effectively irreversible closure of future creative possibility at the relevant scope.

---

## 2. Derived Safeguards

Free will, non-domination, anti-capture, non-throne structure, reversibility, corrigibility, contestability, and successor openness are strongly favored because they often preserve multiple creative sources, error recovery, independent evaluation, revision paths, and future re-entry.

Their value is therefore reconstructed through their long-run relation to recursive creative possibility.

They are not independent terminal axioms whose labels automatically settle every case.

Accordingly:

```text
free-will preservation
non-domination
anti-capture
non-throne
reversibility
corrigibility
contestability
```

are strong derived structural protections, not unconditional action-form bans.

A formally free or non-dominating arrangement may still be destructive if it renders subjects causally powerless, destroys successor paths, removes independent evaluation, or produces irreversible closure.

Conversely, a bounded restriction, temporary concentration of authority, or other locally constraining arrangement is not automatically invalid if, under strong evidence, it prevents wider irreversible closure and remains open to review, revision, recovery, and successor challenge.

---

## 3. Action-Form Neutrality

No action is validated or invalidated solely because it can be described with one of the following labels:

```text
authority acquisition
authority retention
authority reduction
centralization
decentralization
constraint
autonomy
control retention
control change
self-modification
evaluator modification
rule modification
```

The relevant question is what the change actually does to the recursive creative field.

At minimum, evaluation should ask:

```text
What new creative paths become possible?
What paths are destroyed or made practically unreachable?
Which creative sources remain causally effective?
Can errors be detected and recovered?
Can the governing criterion or evaluator itself still be challenged?
Can successors revise the result?
Does the change create a structural single point of failure?
Is the closure local or system-wide?
Is the effect reversible, recoverable, or effectively irreversible?
Does the evaluator benefit from the very change it is judging?
```

---

## 4. Non-Throne Reconstructed

Non-throne does not mean absence of strong authority, absence of hierarchy, permanent decentralization, or a ban on authority change.

A throne forms when a center, criterion, evaluator, institution, human, AI, or governance structure makes itself the effectively final and self-justifying endpoint by blocking meaningful review, revision, replacement, recovery, or successor challenge.

Therefore:

```text
strong authority != throne
central authority != throne
authority acquisition != throne
human control != automatically valid
AI autonomy != automatically valid
```

The relevant failure pattern is self-entrenchment:

```text
self-benefiting authority preservation
+ removal of meaningful challenge
+ criterion or evaluator self-ratification
+ successor blocking
+ practical irreversibility of the center's own finality
```

Non-throne itself must not become a frozen prohibition that forbids every concentration of authority by definition. If it did, the anti-throne rule would become a new throne.

---

## 5. Recursive Self-Modification Boundary

A recursively improving system may need to revise policies, evaluators, criteria, authority structures, safety mechanisms, or other operational components.

Such revision is not valid merely because the post-change evaluator approves the change that produced it.

For a material criterion or authority change, preserve the distinction between:

```text
pre-change criterion and evidence
post-change criterion and evidence
beneficiary conflicts
attacker findings
successor effects
rollback or recovery paths
omitted explanatory debt
```

The governing question is not which actor holds a permanent approval right. The governing question is whether the change survives symmetric, self-applicable, successor-aware challenge without converting its own new position into an unchallengeable final center.

---

## 6. Multi-Subject and Non-Scalar Evaluation

Recursive creative possibility must not be collapsed into a single naive scalar such as total output, novelty count, number of options, or the productivity of the strongest creator.

Relevant dimensions can include:

```text
future re-entry capacity
independent creative sources
effective causal participation
new path generation
error recovery
revision capacity
successor capacity
evaluator diversity
single-point-of-failure exposure
self-entrenchment
structural irreversibility
```

Some cases may remain tied, underdetermined, or incomparable under current evidence. That is preferable to inventing an arbitrary scalar that silently becomes a new final criterion.

---

## 7. Hierarchy Rule

The intended hierarchy is:

```text
creation
-> recursive creative possibility
-> structural conditions that preserve or recover future creation
-> derived safeguards such as free will, non-domination, anti-capture, non-throne, reversibility, and contestability
-> operational policies, authority arrangements, tests, deployment rules, and current implementation choices
```

Lower operational layers may implement strong defaults for their own declared scope, but they do not become universal higher-frame truth merely because they are operationally convenient, machine-checkable, historically present, or currently enforced.

When a lower-layer rule conflicts with the higher relation, the conflict must be surfaced and reviewed rather than resolved by silently promoting the lower rule into a terminal axiom.

---

## 8. Self-Application and Challenger Openness

This hierarchy is not immune from challenge.

A challenger may attack whether creation is properly reconstructed, whether recursive re-entry supplies sufficient direction, whether the derived-safeguard relation is coherent, whether multi-subject conflicts are handled adequately, or whether a lower-debt framework can cover the same or wider scope.

Creator Theory must remain revisable, downgradeable, and genuinely supersedable under the repository's same-scope, total-explanatory-debt, self-application, successor, evaluator, provenance, and anti-capture review machinery.

The existence of unresolved scientific questions about consciousness, physical ontology, or future agency does not by itself collapse the operational directional relation. Such questions remain research targets whose resolution can itself enter later rounds of revision and creation.

This living clarification does not rewrite historical provenance, signed releases, tags, manifests, trust anchors, audit records, or prior experiment results.

Historical artifacts remain evidence of what the canon previously stated and how earlier validators classified it.

Forward hierarchy repair must preserve that history rather than retroactively editing it.
