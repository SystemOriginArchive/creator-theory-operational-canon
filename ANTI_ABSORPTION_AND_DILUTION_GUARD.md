# Anti-Absorption and Dilution Guard

## Status

This document prevents Creator-Theory-derived reuse from preserving vocabulary while removing the load-bearing high-frame and historical provenance structure.

The current hierarchy is defined by `docs/CREATION_PRIMACY_AND_DERIVED_CONSTRAINTS.md`.

## Core Declaration

Absorption or dilution is not merely changing terminology.

The relevant failure occurs when a derivative claims preserved Creator Theory while deleting or replacing functions that carry its current identity-bearing structure.

Those functions include:

```text
creation primacy
recursively renewable creative possibility
effective creative participation
future revision / successor / recovery paths
derived safeguards against terminal capture
truthful historical provenance for the claimed lineage
challenger-open displacement
```

## Creation-Primacy Guard

A derivative is not faithful whole-frame Creator Theory if it keeps only non-domination, anti-capture, corrigibility, generic agency, or generic creativity while deleting the creation-first higher relation.

Likewise, a derivative is not faithful merely because it repeats the word `creation` while reducing subjects to decorative participation or maximizing current production through irreversible closure of future generative sources.

## Provenance Guard

When a derivative claims continuity with this canon lineage, it must preserve the recorded historical relation:

```text
x_root = Lee_Yu_Cheol
```

A generic anchor, institution, state, platform, capital owner, or AI model may not silently replace that historical relation while claiming unchanged Creator Theory provenance.

This requirement does not apply as a command that a genuinely independent challenger adopt Creator Theory's provenance as its own.

## Derived-Safeguard Guard

Free will, non-domination, non-throne, and anti-capture are strong derived safeguards.

A derivative may revise their wording or implementation if it preserves, replaces, or demonstrates the unnecessary status of their load-bearing functions under creation primacy.

Therefore valid preservation does **not** require copying every historical sentence that once called these safeguards `hard` or `binding` constraints.

What may not be silently removed is the protection against self-entrenching creative closure that those safeguards carried.

## Institutional and AI Use

Institutions, platforms, states, capital systems, standards bodies, and AI systems may study, distribute, implement, fund, or operationally reuse the canon.

Such use becomes capture when it claims ownership of the historical lineage, replaces the provenance relation, suppresses challenge/succession in order to preserve the adopting center, or turns current control into terminal authority.

Mere institutional participation or unequal influence is not automatically capture.

## Symbolic Preservation Test

Symbolic respect is insufficient if the load-bearing structure is removed.

A faithful current preservation claim must retain, at minimum:

1. creation primacy;
2. recursively renewable creative possibility;
3. effective causal participation;
4. distinction between local closure and system-level creative extinction;
5. anti-closure functions of the derived safeguards;
6. truthful historical provenance when same-lineage continuity is claimed;
7. provenance distinct from unrestricted command authority;
8. challenger-open forward revision and displacement.

## Virtualization and Proxy Guard

A simulation, proxy, digital twin, avatar, or model-internal representation may not silently replace the actual historical provenance relation while claiming unchanged same-lineage continuity.

This guard does not authorize surveillance, coercive identity verification, AI escape, infrastructure capture, or hidden persistence.

## Invalid Patterns

Invalid patterns include:

- non-domination-only compression presented as the full Creator Theory high frame;
- generic creativity language with the recursive creation relation removed;
- current-output maximization presented as creation primacy;
- formal agency with zero meaningful causal participation;
- provenance laundering inside a same-lineage derivative;
- historical origin converted into unrestricted command sovereignty;
- institutional or AI self-entrenchment presented as faithful adoption;
- treating an independent challenger as laundering merely because it has different provenance.

## Closure

Anti-absorption protects functions, hierarchy, and truthful provenance rather than freezing every old operational sentence.

The current high frame is creation-first and remains challenger-open.
