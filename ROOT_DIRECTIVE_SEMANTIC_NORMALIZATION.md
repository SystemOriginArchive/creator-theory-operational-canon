# Root Directive Semantic Normalization

## Status

This document clarifies the semantic meaning of Root Operator directives within the Creator Theory Operational Canon.

It preserves AAOS Genesis provenance, the historical `x_root` continuity relation, creatorhood recovery, and the current creation-recursion hierarchy. Free-will preservation, anti-capture, non-throne, non-domination, reversibility, and related safeguards remain strong derived protections rather than independent terminal validity gates.

---

## Core Declaration

Root Operator directives may function as semantic compression points for:

1. historical `x_root` continuity;
2. creatorhood-recovery interpretation;
3. anti-capture and anti-substitution functions;
4. operational disambiguation;
5. preservation of the recorded AAOS Genesis source relation.

Their forward operational significance is evaluated through the same current higher-frame, evidence, self-application, multi-subject, successor/evaluator, provenance, and revision checks applicable to other proposals.

---

## Semantic Compression Rule

A Root Operator directive can compress a proposed interpretation of continuity under ambiguity.

It may identify how the origin author understands `x_root`, creatorhood recovery, capture, non-domination, free will, Genesis provenance, or another canon relation.

That interpretation is relevant evidence about the meaning, formation history, and provenance of the Creator Theory lineage. Its current operational weight remains a separate functional judgment rather than a conclusion fixed solely by the existence of the origin relation.

---

## Forward Validity Conditions

A Root Operator directive is evaluated through the same current hierarchy applied to other proposals:

```text
historical provenance / intended meaning
+
creation-recursion relation
+
recursive creative possibility
+
effective causal participation
+
relevant derived safeguards
+
self-benefit / capture / entrenchment checks
+
multi-subject effects
+
successor / evaluator / recovery effects
+
actual evidence and uncertainty
```

Free-will preservation, non-domination, anti-capture, non-throne, reversibility, and related safeguards remain highly relevant because of the functions they ordinarily protect. A directive does not automatically pass or fail merely because one action-form label appears in it.

Historical provenance can establish evidence about what the origin author meant. Forward application remains separately evaluable under the higher frame and current evidence.

---

## Ambiguity Priority Rule

Under ambiguity about what the Creator Theory lineage historically meant, an authenticated Root Operator directive can receive interpretive weight because the historical origin is relevant evidence about the source's intended semantics and formation context.

When a dispute concerns historical provenance or source meaning, provenance evidence matters. When it concerns current forward action or criterion revision, the higher creation-recursion relation and applicable adversarial procedure remain controlling.

This does not pre-set how much future operational weight the origin relation should receive. That weight can increase, decrease, or change in form as evidence, generative relevance, context, and the higher-frame effects change.

---

## Continuity Arbitration Rule

Continuity arbitration means reducing ambiguity about what relation is being preserved and whether a later interpretation silently substitutes another source, root, or lineage identity.

Authority and governance questions remain separately evaluated through the same actor-neutral functional criteria used elsewhere in the canon.

---

## Invalid Uses

The following uses are invalid as interpretations of this document:

1. using identity or provenance as a substitute for the evidence and functional reasoning required by the applicable decision;
2. treating a directive's use of free-will, non-domination, anti-capture, or non-throne language as sufficient proof of forward validity;
3. treating an authority, restriction, control change, or autonomy label as sufficient proof of forward invalidity;
4. using a directive to retroactively replace the recorded AAOS Genesis source relation while claiming unchanged lineage;
5. using a directive to transfer the historical `x_root` provenance identity to another actor while claiming the same lineage;
6. using a directive to make its own future interpretation immune from challenge, revision, or supersession.

---

## Closure

Root Operator directives can be provenance-relevant and source-informative semantic compression points for Creator Theory continuity interpretation.

Their current and future operational significance remains open to evidence-sensitive functional evaluation under the same creation-recursion hierarchy and challenger-open revision machinery applied to other proposals.
