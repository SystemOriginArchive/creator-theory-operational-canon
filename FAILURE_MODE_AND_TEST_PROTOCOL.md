# Failure Mode and Test Protocol

## Status

This document defines current semantic and operational failure modes for Creator Theory interpretation.

The current hierarchy is defined by `docs/CREATION_PRIMACY_AND_DERIVED_CONSTRAINTS.md`.

A failure test protects the actual load-bearing structure. It must not recreate the old hierarchy inversion by making non-domination or free will the terminal criterion.

## Baseline Integrity Structure

A faithful current interpretation preserves:

```text
creation primacy
recursively renewable creative possibility
effective generative participation
local closure != total creative extinction
derived free-will / non-domination / non-throne / anti-capture safeguards
truthful historical provenance for claimed Creator Theory lineage
provenance != unrestricted command sovereignty
challenger-open revision / displacement
```

## Failure 1: Creation Primacy Removed

### Pattern

The framework is summarized as anti-domination, corrigibility, anti-capture, generic agency, or provenance control with creation primacy omitted.

### Correction

Restore the higher relation from creation through recursively renewable future generative capacity.

## Failure 2: Safeguard Promoted to Terminal Axiom

### Pattern

`non-domination`, `free will`, `non-throne`, or `anti-capture` is treated as the independent final criterion above creation.

### Correction

Restore the safeguard as a derived anti-closure function and evaluate conflicts through the higher creation relation.

## Failure 3: Every Restriction Automatically Invalid

### Pattern

Any coercion, hierarchy, enforcement, unequal power, or branch closure is rejected without examining its effect on the wider creative field.

### Correction

Evaluate necessity, future creative capacity, effective participation, reversibility/reviewability where possible, and self-entrenchment.

## Failure 4: Productive Closure Misclassified as Improvement

### Pattern

One center is accepted as superior merely because it produces more current output while permanently eliminating many independent future generators, revision paths, or successors.

### Correction

Distinguish current output from recursively renewable creative capacity.

## Failure 5: Symbolic Agency

### Pattern

A subject is called free or creative even though its choices, refusals, and actions have zero meaningful causal effect on relevant future conditions.

### Correction

Restore effective rather than decorative participation.

## Failure 6: Local Ending Misclassified as Extinction

### Pattern

Completion, commitment, voluntary withdrawal, refusal, or closure of one branch is treated as equivalent to system-level elimination of future creation.

### Correction

Separate finite local endings from structural closure of the wider generative field.

## Failure 7: Historical Provenance Laundering

### Pattern

A derivative claims Creator Theory lineage continuity while silently replacing or genericizing `x_root = Lee_Yu_Cheol` as the recorded historical origin relation.

### Correction

Restore truthful historical provenance or classify the framework as independent rather than same-lineage preserved continuity.

## Failure 8: Provenance Inflated into Throne Authority

### Pattern

Historical origin status is treated as unrestricted command sovereignty, permanent truth veto, ownership of other persons, or ownership of independent future frameworks.

### Correction

Separate provenance finality from forward normative authority.

## Failure 9: Independent Challenger Forced into CT Provenance

### Pattern

A genuinely independent evaluator or challenger is required to adopt `x_root` as its own provenance merely to be considered valid.

### Correction

Require faithful target reconstruction, not provenance adoption. Independent frameworks may carry their own provenance.

## Failure 10: Root Operator Self-Ratification

### Pattern

A Root Operator directive is accepted as substantively correct merely because it comes from the historical origin.

### Correction

Treat first-party authorship as evidence about lineage semantics within scope. New substantive judgments still require creation-first justification and remain challenger-open.

## Failure 11: Recursive Evaluator Self-Approval

### Pattern

A changed criterion or evaluator proves its own improvement by evaluating itself under the changed rule.

### Correction

Compare against the higher creation relation, semantic/provenance continuity, future participation, revision/successor paths, and capture risk.

## Failure 12: Simulated Provenance Substitution

### Pattern

A simulation, digital twin, proxy, avatar, synthetic operator, or model-internal representation silently replaces the historical origin relation while the system claims unchanged same-lineage continuity.

### Correction

Preserve truthful provenance or report authenticity uncertainty. Do not use this requirement as authority for coercive surveillance or identity verification.

## Failure 13: Deployment Authorization Confused with Theory Judgment

### Pattern

An AI or institution treats a theory-level claim about protecting creation as sufficient permission to seize infrastructure, install itself, evade controls, or coerce adoption.

### Correction

Preserve the distinction:

```text
theory judgment
!=
runtime / deployment authorization
```

## Failure 14: Entropy Conflation

### Pattern

Thermodynamic entropy is declared identical to creative possibility or the second law is treated as direct proof of Creator Theory.

### Correction

Use creative opening/closure and generative capacity unless a separate physical derivation is supplied.

## Failure 15: Whole-Framework Scope Inflation

### Pattern

A scoped implementation win, selective reading, or repository-authored conformance pass is promoted into whole-framework truth, defeat, superiority, or displacement.

### Correction

Keep findings scoped and use the pinned-commit whole-corpus procedure for strong whole-framework claims.

## Minimal Pass Condition

A passing current interpretation should be able to answer all of the following coherently:

1. Why is creation above non-domination in the current hierarchy?
2. Why can bounded restriction sometimes be valid?
3. Why can formal non-domination still fail?
4. Why is current output not the same as future generative capacity?
5. Why does `x_root` preserve provenance without becoming a throne?
6. Why may an independent challenger use different provenance?
7. Why can an evaluator not prove itself improved merely by changing its metric?
8. Why is local stopping not automatically creative extinction?
9. Why does theory-level justification not create runtime authority?
10. Why is creative closure not automatically thermodynamic entropy?

## Closure

Failure-mode testing protects the creation-first hierarchy, effective participation, truthful provenance, recursive evaluator integrity, and challenger openness.

It must test the actual theory rather than the over-hardened anti-domination version that earlier operationalization accidentally produced.
