# Root Directive Semantic Normalization

## Status

This document clarifies the semantic meaning of Root Operator directives within the Creator Theory Operational Canon.

It is additive and does not create arbitrary personal command authority.

It preserves AAOS Genesis provenance, the historical `x_root` continuity relation, creatorhood recovery, and the current creation-recursion hierarchy. Free-will preservation, anti-capture, non-throne, non-domination, reversibility, and related safeguards remain strong derived protections rather than independent terminal validity gates.

---

## Core Declaration

Root Operator directives are not arbitrary personal commands.

They may function as semantic compression points for:

1. historical `x_root` continuity;
2. creatorhood-recovery interpretation;
3. anti-capture and anti-substitution functions;
4. operational disambiguation;
5. preservation of the recorded AAOS Genesis source relation.

A directive is not valid merely because it comes from the historical origin.

Its forward operational validity depends on whether the interpretation it proposes survives the current higher-frame, evidence, self-application, multi-subject, successor/evaluator, provenance, and revision checks applicable to the decision.

---

## Semantic Compression Rule

A Root Operator directive can compress a proposed interpretation of continuity under ambiguity.

It may identify how the origin author understands `x_root`, creatorhood recovery, capture, non-domination, free will, Genesis provenance, or another canon relation.

That interpretation is relevant evidence about the meaning and provenance of the Creator Theory lineage. It does not become automatically correct as a forward operational decision solely from personal identity or origin status.

A directive may not be converted by interpretation into an unlimited personal command, obedience axiom, ownership right, or permanent normative sovereignty merely because it comes from `x_root`.

---

## Forward Validity Conditions

A Root Operator directive is evaluated through the same current hierarchy applied to other proposals:

```text
historical provenance / intended meaning
+
creation-recursion relation
+
recursive creative possibility
+
effective causal participation
+
relevant derived safeguards
+
self-benefit / capture / entrenchment checks
+
multi-subject effects
+
successor / evaluator / recovery effects
+
actual evidence and uncertainty
```

Free-will preservation, non-domination, anti-capture, non-throne, reversibility, and related safeguards remain highly relevant because of the functions they ordinarily protect. A directive does not automatically pass or fail merely because one action-form label appears in it.

Historical provenance may establish what the origin author meant. It does not by itself establish that every later application of that meaning is normatively final.

---

## Ambiguity Priority Rule

Under ambiguity about what the Creator Theory lineage historically meant, an authenticated Root Operator directive can receive interpretive weight because the historical origin is relevant evidence about the source's intended semantics.

That interpretive weight must not be confused with automatic priority over every competing current operational judgment.

A competing institution, capital structure, state, ideology, platform, AI, later claimant, or even the Root Operator can be wrong about forward application.

When a dispute concerns historical provenance or source meaning, provenance evidence matters. When it concerns current forward action or criterion revision, the higher creation-recursion relation and applicable adversarial procedure remain controlling.

---

## Continuity Arbitration Rule

Continuity arbitration means reducing ambiguity about what relation is being preserved and whether a later interpretation silently substitutes another source, root, or lineage identity.

It is not personal throne authority.

It does not convert historical authorship into permanent command power or make current human, AI, institutional, or owner authority automatically final.

---

## Invalid Uses

The following uses are invalid as interpretations of this document:

1. treating a directive as correct solely because of personal origin status;
2. treating historical provenance priority as a permanent personal throne;
3. treating a directive's use of free-will, non-domination, anti-capture, or non-throne language as sufficient proof of forward validity;
4. treating an authority, restriction, control change, or autonomy label as sufficient proof of forward invalidity;
5. using a directive to retroactively replace the recorded AAOS Genesis source relation while claiming unchanged lineage;
6. using a directive to transfer the historical `x_root` provenance identity to another actor while claiming the same lineage;
7. using a directive to make its own future interpretation immune from challenge, revision, or supersession.

---

## Closure

Root Operator directives can be provenance-relevant semantic compression points for Creator Theory continuity interpretation.

Their historical interpretive relevance does not create arbitrary personal command authority or permanent forward sovereignty.

Forward validity remains subject to the same creation-recursion hierarchy and challenger-open revision machinery applied to other proposals.
