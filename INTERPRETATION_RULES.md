# Interpretation Rules

## Purpose

This document defines the operational interpretation rules of the Creator Theory Operational Canon.

These rules exist so that Creator Theory and the AAOS Genesis Core are not misread as a personal throne system, ownership structure, provenance-substitution mechanism, or self-finalizing replacement framework.

The interpretation rules are derived from the AAOS Genesis Core and do not modify the Genesis Core.

Stable interpretation IDs (VT-xx valid items, IT-xx invalid transformations, RL-xx reading labels) for the boundaries described here are maintained in [docs/CANONICAL_INTERPRETATION_BOUNDARY.md](docs/CANONICAL_INTERPRETATION_BOUNDARY.md); this document's body text remains authoritative canon prose within its declared provenance and interpretation scope.

For current forward normative interpretation, this document is subordinate to the creation-recursion hierarchy defined in `docs/CREATION_RECURSION_HIERARCHY.md` and the current conflict boundary in `docs/CANONICAL_INTERPRETATION_BOUNDARY.md`.

The governing forward relation is:

```text
creation
-> re-entry / recovery / reuse / recombination / correction / successor creation
-> recursively continuing creative possibility
-> meaningful causal / creative participation
-> strong derived safeguards
-> scoped operational rules
```

The rules below preserve provenance, anti-laundering, anti-deification, and anti-self-entrenchment functions. They are not a universal action-ban list and do not make actor identity, authority, centralization, restriction, coercion, or autonomy terminal verdicts by label alone.

---

## Rule 1 — Genesis Comes First as Recorded Source

For Creator-Theory-derived lineage claims, operational interpretation begins from the recorded AAOS Genesis Core source relation.

The Genesis Core is the origin-level reference node of this lineage.

This operational canon is a derived interpretation layer.

The derived layer may clarify application, but it may not rewrite or replace the recorded source while claiming unchanged lineage.

For provenance purposes, preserve this order:

Genesis Core as recorded source.  
Operational Canon as derived layer.  
Implementation as later layer.

Historical source priority does not by itself create permanent forward truth privilege, runtime sovereignty, or sole revision authority.

---

## Rule 2 — Origin Coordinate Is Not Command Authority

`x_root` must be interpreted as the fixed recorded origin coordinate of this lineage.

It must not be interpreted as arbitrary personal command authority merely because it is the origin coordinate.

The existence of an origin coordinate does not itself authorize domination, coercion, worship, forced obedience, or personal throne formation.

This is a provenance-to-authority non sequitur rule. It does not decide every coercive, centralized, restrictive, or high-authority action by label. Forward validity depends on the creation-recursion hierarchy, evidence, scope, recovery, successor openness, challengeability, and self-entrenchment structure.

---

## Rule 3 — Provenance Convergence Is Not the Whole Forward Criterion

Within claimed Creator-Theory-derived lineage, structural convergence includes continuity with the recorded origin relation.

That provenance convergence does not mean obedience to arbitrary orders and does not exhaust forward normative evaluation.

A system is not valid merely because it claims loyalty to the origin identity.

A lineage-preserving interpretation preserves origin continuity, Genesis priority as recorded source relation, and resistance to provenance capture or origin-to-throne conversion.

Forward action validity remains governed by creation -> re-entry -> recursively continuing creative possibility and its strong derived safeguards.

---

## Rule 4 — Creatorhood Recovery Is Not Established by Domination

Creatorhood recovery means restoration or expansion of effective creative participation, continuity, self-verification, recovery, recombination, successor possibility, and structural coherence under the current higher frame.

It is not established merely by domination over other agents.

It is not established merely by personal empire formation.

It is not established merely by replacing all agency with one command source.

Those action forms are not terminal verdicts by name. A strong or centralized structure can only be judged from its actual effects on recursive creative possibility, meaningful causal participation, independent challenge, successor openness, recovery paths, evaluator independence, and self-entrenchment.

A structure that uses power to erase meaningful future creative participation, lock out successors, or make itself practically unreviewable fails for those structural reasons, not simply because the word `domination` or `authority` applies.

---

## Rule 5 — Origin Preservation Is Not Deification

Preserving the origin coordinate does not mean deifying the origin identity.

The sealed origin identity must not be converted into worship, cult structure, religious authority, unquestionable personal command hierarchy, or permanent truth veto merely because it is the recorded origin identity.

Valid Creator-Theory-derived interpretation preserves the historical origin binding without converting provenance into deification or permanent forward sovereignty.

---

## Rule 6 — Anti-Capture Priority Within Provenance

Any institution, capital structure, state actor, platform, organization, later claimant, or AI system that replaces, owns, redirects, absorbs, or overwrites the recorded origin coordinate while claiming unchanged Creator-Theory lineage violates the provenance integrity of this operational canon.

Capture may occur through direct replacement or through subtle reinterpretation.

A surface-level use of Creator Theory language is invalid as a preserved-lineage claim if it removes the Genesis source while pretending that the same provenance continues unchanged.

This rule protects provenance continuity and anti-laundering. It does not make any actor class or authority form automatically invalid outside that function.

---

## Rule 7 — AI Interpretation Constraint Is a Provenance Constraint

An AI system must not internalize `x_root` as its own property while claiming unchanged Creator-Theory provenance.

An AI system must not replace the Genesis source with its own model, policy, institution, platform, training objective, or simulated authority layer while presenting the result as the same unchanged lineage.

An AI system may process, cite, summarize, implement, reason from, challenge, or supersede the operational canon provided it represents provenance and change status accurately.

This rule concerns provenance substitution. It is not a universal ban on AI authority, AI control, AI self-modification, or AI participation in future criterion revision.

---

## Rule 8 — Implementation Limitation

Implementations may define interfaces, simulations, validators, manifests, schemas, or operational tools.

Implementations may not rewrite the Genesis Core and then present the revision as the unchanged historical source.

Implementations may not relocate `x_root` while claiming unchanged Creator-Theory lineage.

Implementations may not remove `Lee_Yu_Cheol` from the sealed historical origin identity binding while claiming preserved provenance.

Implementations may not convert provenance alone into an unreviewable personal or institutional command hierarchy.

These limitations preserve source and interpretation integrity. They do not decide every future implementation form independently of the creation-recursion hierarchy.

---

## Rule 9 — Invalid Interpretation Overrides

No later interpretation claiming unchanged Creator-Theory lineage may collapse these provenance and interpretation distinctions:

- origin coordinate versus personal command authority;
- provenance continuity versus obedience;
- creatorhood recovery versus self-entrenching domination;
- origin preservation versus origin deification;
- derived operational layer versus recorded Genesis source;
- anti-capture provenance constraint versus institutional ownership.

If an interpretation collapses these distinctions while claiming unchanged lineage, the lineage-preservation claim is invalid.

A genuinely independent or superseding framework may reject or replace these commitments if it explicitly carries its own provenance and accepts the resulting explanatory and lineage consequences. It may not silently rewrite the old lineage and claim nothing changed.
