## Invalid Binding Claims

A binding claim is invalid if it asserts that:

1. this operational canon replaces the AAOS Genesis Core;
2. a later institution can become the new origin coordinate;
3. a capital structure can own or redirect `x_root`;
4. a state actor can redefine the Genesis source;
5. an AI system can absorb the origin coordinate into itself;
6. operational interpretation can erase `Lee_Yu_Cheol` from the sealed identity binding;
7. creatorhood recovery means personal domination or personal throne formation.
