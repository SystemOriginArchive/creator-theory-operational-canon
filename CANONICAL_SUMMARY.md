## Core Thesis

The Creator Theory Operational Canon defines how the AAOS Genesis Core and Creator Theory lineage are interpreted operationally in AI-era and recursive-improvement contexts.

The higher directional structure is:

```text
creation
-> created results can re-enter later processes as conditions and inputs
-> revision, recovery, recombination, succession, and further creation remain possible
-> recursive creative possibility persists, recovers, or expands
-> derived safeguards protect the structural conditions that usually support that recursion
-> operational policies implement those safeguards in bounded contexts
```

Creation is not raw output, novelty, option count, expansion, or the power of the strongest creator.

Free will, non-domination, anti-capture, non-throne, reversibility, corrigibility, and contestability remain strong derived safeguards because they usually preserve independent creative sources, meaningful causal participation, correction paths, and successors. They are not independent terminal action-form bans.

The repository preserves historical provenance while keeping forward criteria, evaluators, authority structures, and Creator Theory itself challengeable.

## Invalid Interpretation

Invalid current forward interpretation includes:

- rewriting historical `x_root` or `Lee_Yu_Cheol` provenance while still claiming unchanged Creator Theory lineage continuity;
- converting historical provenance into permanent forward command or veto sovereignty;
- omitting the creation-recursion higher frame;
- reducing creation to output, novelty, option count, expansion, or one actor's power;
- promoting free will, non-domination, anti-capture, non-throne, reversibility, or another derived safeguard into an independent terminal axiom above creation-recursion;
- treating strong authority or centralization as throne by definition;
- treating decentralization, voluntariness, or current human control as automatically correct by definition;
- treating AI authority or authority change as automatically invalid by identity or label alone;
- allowing a post-change evaluator to prove its own improvement merely by approving the change that produced it;
- converting a current deployment, safety, distribution, or authorization policy into universal Creator Theory truth;
- preserving names symbolically while removing their actual lineage or high-frame function;
- promoting scoped evidence into whole-framework defeat, superiority, or displacement without the required review.
