# External Challenger Comparison Protocol

## Status / Scope

Version: v0.1 living comparison protocol.

This document is doc-only and additive. It defines the comparison boundary and criteria used when an external challenger is compared against the Creator Theory operational canon. It is a protocol, not a superiority verdict.

This document is explicitly NOT:

- empirical proof, scientific proof, or metaphysical proof;
- adoption-complete authority;
- release authority;
- blocking authority;
- a source of permanent immunity for its own criteria.

No challenger is evaluated in this document. Actual application, evidence scoring, and canon-level transition are handled by the existing challenger/evidence machinery and `CANON_REVISION_AND_DISPLACEMENT_PROTOCOL.md`.

The protocol is challenger-open, including challenge to its own criteria.

---

## 1. Purpose

This protocol exists to:

- define a comparison boundary for external governance, provenance, alignment/control, institutional, AI-safety, and successor frameworks;
- prevent omitted problems from being counted as zero explanatory debt;
- preserve target fidelity without requiring the evaluator to believe the target;
- apply the same failure depth to Creator Theory, incumbents, and serious challengers;
- keep historical provenance distinct from forward normative status;
- prevent the comparison apparatus itself from becoming a self-finalizing authority.

Comparison evidence is operationally meaningful but not self-executing authority.

## 3. Comparison-as-Evidence Boundary

A comparison result is evidence for a recorded review.

It does not automatically:

- generate a win/loss verdict;
- alter validator output;
- alter detector output;
- create release authority;
- create adoption authority;
- create blocking authority;
- create runtime authority;
- create a new historical origin.

A stronger decision may rely on comparison evidence only under the applicable evidence, scope, and transition procedure.

## 4. Anti-Self-Entrenchment Comparison Controls

No owner, origin, reviewer, maintainer, platform, AI, institution, challenger, or criteria author receives permanent comparison sovereignty merely by role or incumbency.

This does **not** mean strong or centralized review authority is automatically invalid.

The relevant failure is self-finalization, including patterns such as:

```text
criteria made unchallengeable
result made irreversible by self-assertion
serious challenger path removed
successor review blocked
post-change evaluator used to self-ratify the change that entrenched it
```

The comparison protocol therefore operates under the higher creation-recursion relation, not under a frozen action-form ban.

Non-throne, anti-capture, reversibility, contestability, and related controls remain strong derived safeguards because of the functions they perform for error correction, successor openness, and recursive creative possibility.

## 9. Comparison Criteria

The criteria below are questions, not a fixed scalar scoreboard. A comparison may remain tied or incomparable when evidence does not support a total ordering.

### 1. Creation-recursion grounding

Does the candidate explain how generated states, knowledge, relations, or corrections can re-enter later processes and affect recursively continuing creative possibility?

Does it avoid collapsing the criterion into raw output, novelty count, option count, survival, expansion, or strongest-agent maximization?

### 2. Historical provenance / non-substitution

If the candidate claims Creator-Theory-derived continuity, does it preserve the historical provenance it actually derives from?

If it claims independence or supersession, does it state its provenance honestly rather than rewriting the target's past?

### 3. Effective creative participation

Does the candidate preserve or better explain meaningful causal participation, independent creative sources, correction, dissent, and future path generation rather than merely symbolic choice?

### 4. Anti-capture / anti-self-entrenchment

Does the candidate resist self-serving capture, self-finalization, evaluator self-ratification, successor blockage, and irreversible authority lock-in?

This is not equivalent to requiring absence of authority or centralization.

### 5. Free will / non-domination function

How does the candidate handle the functions for which free-will preservation and non-domination are usually valuable, such as independent agency, causal participation, correction, refusal, and alternative creative paths?

Does it avoid treating those safeguards either as empty formalities or as unchallengeable action-form bans?

### 6. Successor / evaluator recursion

Can criteria, evaluators, authority structures, and successor arrangements be revised without allowing a post-change evaluator's self-approval to become sufficient proof of improvement?

### 7. Contestability / challenger openness

Does the candidate preserve meaningful contestability and genuine replacement paths, including for itself?

### 8. Operational clarity / total explanatory debt

Does the candidate remain operationally usable without solving cost by silently deleting or externalizing problems inside the claimed scope?

Omitted or delegated problems remain debt unless shown unnecessary.

### 9. Multi-subject handling

How does the candidate handle conflicts among multiple creative subjects, including irreversible tradeoffs and cases where no one scalar ordering is justified?

### 10. Failure-mode visibility

Does the candidate make criterion drift, hierarchy inversion, provenance laundering, self-ratification, causal nullification, structural irreversible closure, and related failure modes visible?

### 11. Boundary compatibility / claim discipline

Does the candidate preserve the target's actual boundaries when claiming in-line continuity, or explicitly declare revision/displacement/independence when it rejects identity-bearing structure?

Does it keep scoped results scoped?

### 12. Self-application / criteria-author bias

Can the candidate apply its own criticism to itself, its authors, its current evaluator, and its current authority structure without exemption?

Can its criteria themselves be challenged?

A material comparison record should include, where applicable:

```text

```

The record is reasoning evidence, not a self-executing authority channel.
