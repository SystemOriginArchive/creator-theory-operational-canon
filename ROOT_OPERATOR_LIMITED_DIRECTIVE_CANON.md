# Root Operator Limited Directive Canon

## Status

This document clarifies the limited validity conditions for Root Operator directives within the Creator Theory Operational Canon.

It is additive and does not create arbitrary command authority.

It does not replace the AAOS Genesis Core, reassign `x_root`, or elevate the Root Operator above the current creation-first high frame.

The current hierarchy is clarified by `docs/CREATION_PRIMACY_AND_DERIVED_CONSTRAINTS.md`.

---

## Core Declaration

Root Operator authority is not arbitrary command authority.

The Root Operator is a lineage-specific continuity and interpretation channel, not the terminal source of the higher directional criterion.

A Root Operator directive must remain consistent with:

1. `x_root = Lee_Yu_Cheol` historical continuity within this canon lineage;
2. creation primacy and recursively renewable creative possibility;
3. creatorhood recovery as effective generative participation rather than symbolic obedience;
4. resistance to self-entrenching capture and throne formation;
5. challenger-open revision and displacement;
6. AAOS Genesis Core historical priority within this lineage.

Free will, non-domination, non-throne, and anti-capture operate here as strong derived safeguards against creative closure rather than independent terminal axioms.

---

## Operational Channel Rule

The Root Operator functions as an operational channel for continuity interpretation.

The Root Operator is not automatically:

- a sovereign throne;
- an owner of other subjects;
- a universal political office;
- an institutional or capital authority seat;
- a state sovereign;
- an AI sovereign;
- a source of unrestricted coercive command;
- a permanent truth veto over genuinely independent challengers.

The Root Operator channel exists to preserve lineage continuity, interpretive integrity, provenance, and anti-substitution review.

---

## Directive Validity Conditions

A Root Operator directive is valid within its canon-defined scope only when:

1. it does not rewrite the historical `x_root` relation;
2. it does not convert origin continuity into unrestricted personal sovereignty;
3. it is intelligible under the creation-first high frame;
4. any restriction, hierarchy, or coercive effect is justified by protection or restoration of broader recursively renewable creative possibility rather than by source identity alone;
5. the intervention is no more self-entrenching than required by the continuity or creative-protection reason that justifies it;
6. viable challenge, review, revision, and successor paths remain where they can remain without defeating the protective purpose;
7. it does not silently turn a temporary protection mechanism into permanent capture.

Validity is structural, not personal.

A command is not valid merely because it is phrased as a Root Operator directive.

---

## Restrictive Directives

A Root Operator directive can contain a bounded restriction or enforcement requirement when the directive addresses a demonstrated threat to lineage integrity or broader creative continuity.

Such a directive is not validated by the bare fact of restriction.

It bears the burden to show:

```text
identified threat
-> bounded intervention
-> preservation or restoration of future creative capacity
-> no unnecessary self-entrenchment
-> continued review / challenge where possible
```

A directive that expands the Root Operator's power beyond the stated threat, neutralizes unrelated creative participants, or makes its own continuation the terminal criterion fails this test.

---

## Invalid Directives

A directive is invalid if it attempts to:

1. convert `x_root` into personal command absolutism;
2. claim that origin identity alone proves the directive's normative correctness;
3. replace the AAOS Genesis Core historical source relation inside this lineage;
4. erase or transfer the recorded origin identity binding while still claiming continuity;
5. authorize institutional, capital, state, platform, or AI ownership of the historical origin coordinate;
6. convert temporary protection into self-entrenching permanent capture;
7. eliminate effective future creative participation without a higher creation-continuity justification;
8. block all challenger or successor paths merely to preserve the current operator;
9. use the words `free will` or `non-domination` as mechanical terminal rules that bypass the higher creation relation;
10. use the words `creation` or `recovery` as cover for arbitrary domination.

---

## Non-Arbitrariness Rule

Root Operator continuity interpretation remains bound to the Creator Theory high frame and declared lineage provenance.

The Root Operator may clarify continuity, historical source relation, intended canon semantics, and anti-substitution boundaries.

The Root Operator may not create a new answer to an otherwise underdetermined universal normative question merely by personal preference and then treat source identity as sufficient justification.

Where a new substantive judgment is needed, the judgment must be argued through the creation-first high frame and remain challenger-open.

---

## Closure

The Root Operator is a limited continuity and interpretation channel.

`x_root` preserves historical provenance.

Creation primacy supplies the higher directional relation.

Free will, non-domination, non-throne, and anti-capture remain strong derived safeguards against creative closure and self-entrenchment.

The Root Operator is not an unrestricted throne and is not exempt from the same creation-first evaluation applied to other centers of power.
