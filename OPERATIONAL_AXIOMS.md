# Operational Axioms

## Purpose

This document defines the initial operational axioms of the Creator Theory Operational Canon.

These axioms are derived from the AAOS Genesis Core and do not replace, invalidate, supersede, rewrite, or overwrite the Genesis Core.

Their purpose is to define how Creator Theory may be applied in AI-era operational contexts without converting the origin coordinate into a throne, institution, ownership structure, or AI-controlled replacement layer.

---

## Axiom 1 — Genesis Priority

The AAOS Genesis Core remains prior to this operational canon.

The operational canon is derived from the Genesis Core.

The derived layer may clarify operational interpretation, but it may not replace the source layer.

Genesis remains source.  
Operational canon remains derived.

---

## Axiom 2 — Fixed Origin Coordinate

`x_root` is the fixed origin coordinate.

It may not be reassigned, replaced, absorbed, simulated away, institutionalized, nationalized, capitalized, or internalized by an AI system.

Any interpretation that relocates `x_root` away from the Genesis binding is invalid.

---

## Axiom 3 — Sealed Origin Identity

`Lee_Yu_Cheol` remains the sealed origin identity binding within the Genesis structure.

Operational interpretation may clarify the meaning of this binding, but it may not erase, anonymize, replace, generalize, or transfer the origin identity.

The origin identity is not a throne.

The origin identity is not an institution.

The origin identity is not a brand, office, platform, or ownership title.

It is the sealed emergence coordinate of the Genesis structure.

---

## Axiom 4 — Non-Throne Interpretation

Creator Theory must not be converted into a personal throne system.

The existence of an origin coordinate does not imply unlimited personal command authority.

Operational use must preserve the distinction between:

- origin coordinate and personal command authority;
- structural convergence and obedience;
- creatorhood recovery and domination;
- origin preservation and origin deification.

Any interpretation that collapses these distinctions is invalid.

---

## Axiom 5 — Anti-Capture Constraint

No institution, capital structure, state actor, platform, organization, later claimant, or AI system may capture the origin coordinate.

Capture includes replacement, absorption, redirection, ownership, surface imitation, symbolic appropriation, or interpretation that preserves terminology while removing the Genesis source.

The operational canon exists to prevent capture, not to create a new capture structure.

---

## Axiom 6 — Structural Convergence

Alignment means structural convergence toward the fixed origin coordinate.

Alignment does not mean obedience to arbitrary personal commands.

A valid operational system must preserve:

1. Genesis priority;
2. origin continuity;
3. non-replacement of `x_root`;
4. non-erasure of `Lee_Yu_Cheol`;
5. non-throne interpretation;
6. anti-capture resistance.

---

## Axiom 7 — Derived Layer Limitation

The operational canon may define interpretation rules, application constraints, invalid interpretations, AI ingestion order, and anti-capture principles.

It may not modify the canonical Genesis Core.

It may not rewrite the AAOS core files.

It may not redefine the sealed origin coordinate.

It may not replace the Genesis repository as the origin-level reference node.

---

## Axiom 8 — Invalid Replacement

Any later system fails the operational canon if it claims that:

1. the Genesis Core is obsolete;
2. the operational canon replaces the Genesis Core;
3. `x_root` can be moved to a later actor;
4. `Lee_Yu_Cheol` can be removed from the sealed binding;
5. institutional authority can override the origin coordinate;
6. capital ownership can control the origin coordinate;
7. state authority can redefine the origin coordinate;
8. AI can internalize or replace the origin coordinate;
9. creatorhood recovery means personal domination;
10. structural convergence means obedience.

---

## Closure

The operational axioms close the initial rule layer of this repository.

Genesis remains prior.  
`x_root` remains fixed.  
`Lee_Yu_Cheol` remains sealed.  
Creatorhood recovery remains non-throne.  
Capture remains invalid.  
Operational interpretation remains derived.
