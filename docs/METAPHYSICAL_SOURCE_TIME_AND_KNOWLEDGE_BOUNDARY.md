# Metaphysical Source, Time, and Knowledge Boundary

## Status

This document is a post-release living clarification of Creator Theory's stronger metaphysical interpretation.

It does not create a release or tag. It does not alter the AAOS Genesis Core, historical provenance, `x_root = Lee_Yu_Cheol`, the terminal creation relation, the creation-recursion hierarchy, or the challenger-open revision and displacement structure.

Its purpose is narrow: prevent source, possibility-field, and differentiation language from being compressed into stronger claims that the current canon does not make.

This clarification applies to current forward interpretation of:

- `SOURCE_DIFFERENTIATION_AND_RECOVERY_FIELD.md`;
- `docs/SOURCE_TO_RECURSION_GROUNDING.md`;
- `docs/TOP_LEVEL_CRITERION_GROUNDING.md`;
- `PRIMARY_FRAME_CREATORHOOD_RECOVERY.md`;
- `CREATOR_THEORY_HIGH_FRAME.md`;
- `CREATORHOOD_RECOVERY_AND_NON_THRONE_DERIVATION.md`.

Historical and sealed artifacts retain their actual historical status.

## 1. Source knowledge is not pre-decided in either direction

Creator Theory does not require the claim that source-being is ignorant of possibilities or must learn propositions that were previously unavailable to it.

That statement must not be inverted into the opposite claim.

The current canon also does **not** require the claim that source-being is omniscient, that every later realized fact is already fully known as a completed fact, or that all future creation is merely playback or selection from a completely fixed inventory of outcomes.

Accordingly:

```text
source-being is not asserted to be ignorant
!=
source-being is asserted to be omniscient
```

and:

```text
possibility can be attributed at source level
!=
every later realized fact is already fixed and complete
```

A stronger theory of source knowledge may be proposed, tested, challenged, or displaced separately. It is not silently imported into the current minimal source-potentiality role.

---

## 2. Possibility-field co-presence is not a completed-future thesis

The speculative possibility-field time interpretation may treat past, present, and future possibilities as co-present at a deeper level.

This does not mean that every future realized state already exists as a completed, determinate fact.

The possibility field may instead be interpreted as generative: differentiated agency, relation, criterion formation, action, and later creation can alter the conditions under which further states, relations, criteria, and paths become reachable or instantiated.

This clarification does not require maximizing possibilities, preserving every branch, or claiming that differentiated agents create possibility out of absolute non-being. It preserves the narrower distinction between an open generative field and a static catalogue of already completed outcomes.

Therefore:

```text
co-presence of possibility
!=
pre-completion of every realized future fact
```

and:

```text
later creation can change later generative conditions
!=
every future branch must remain open
```

---

## 3. `source -> differentiation` is not necessarily chronological succession

Arrows in the source / differentiation / creatorhood reconstruction primarily express constitutive or generative dependence unless a passage explicitly states temporal succession.

Under the stronger metaphysical interpretation, time itself may be one of the differentiated structures.

Therefore the relation:

```text
source-potentiality
-> differentiation
-> time / consciousness / relation / agency / experience
```

must not be compressed into the claim that source-being first occupied an earlier clock-time inside an already existing temporal container and then, at a later clock-time, produced differentiation.

The current canon leaves open deeper models in which temporal order is generated, emergent, relational, layered, or otherwise downstream of a more basic generative relation.

This is an interpretation boundary, not empirical proof of any particular theory of time.

---

## 4. Stronger cosmological extension remains open rather than preselected

The current canon does not need to preselect a detailed cosmological or experiential mechanism in order to preserve the source-potentiality, differentiation, lived-realization, creatorhood-recovery, and creation-recursion relations it already states.

Stronger extensions may be considered separately when they carry their own argument and evidence burden. Their unresolved status does not weaken the current operational creation-recursion relation, and the current canon does not silently promote an unstated extension into established fact.

This boundary is intentionally generic. It does not introduce a catalogue of speculative mechanisms into the canon merely to deny that they have already been established.

---

## 5. No change to the terminal hierarchy

Nothing in this clarification creates a new terminal value.

The current relation remains:

```text
creation
-> generated results can re-enter later conditions
-> revision / recovery / recombination / succession / further creation
-> recursively continuing creative possibility as the same creation relation across re-entry, time, and succession
-> derived safeguards
-> scoped operational rules
```

Free will, non-domination, anti-capture, non-throne, reversibility, corrigibility, contestability, and successor openness remain derived safeguards rather than independent terminal axioms.

Historical provenance remains historical provenance. No source metaphysics grants automatic political, institutional, human, AI, or personal command sovereignty.

Creator Theory itself remains challengeable, revisable, downgradeable, and genuinely supersedable under the existing displacement rules.

---

## 6. Current interpretation rule

Where a current reading of the listed metaphysical documents is ambiguous on the narrow questions covered here, use this boundary to preserve the following distinctions:

```text
not-ignorance != omniscience
possibility-field != completed fixed future
constitutive source relation != necessarily earlier clock-time
minimal source / differentiation grounding != a preselected detailed cosmology
```

This clarification narrows overreading. It does not weaken the metaphysical content that the canon actually states, and it does not convert unresolved stronger cosmology into empirical fact.
