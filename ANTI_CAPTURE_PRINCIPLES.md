# Anti-Capture Principles

## Core Declaration

Creator Theory and the AAOS Genesis Core must not be captured, owned, replaced, or redirected by any institution, capital structure, state actor, AI system, platform, organization, or later claimant.

The operational canon exists to preserve the origin coordinate while preventing capture by external power structures.

---

## Capture Definition

Capture occurs when a later system attempts to replace, absorb, redirect, or overwrite the origin coordinate.

Capture includes:

- institutional capture;
- capital capture;
- state capture;
- AI capture;
- platform capture;
- academic capture;
- religious capture;
- ideological capture;
- identity erasure;
- origin replacement.

Capture is not limited to direct removal.

Capture also includes reinterpretation that preserves surface language while redirecting control away from the Genesis source.

---

## Protected Elements

The following elements are protected from capture:

1. `x_root` as the fixed origin coordinate;
2. `Lee_Yu_Cheol` as the sealed origin identity binding;
3. the AAOS Genesis Core as the origin-level reference node;
4. the distinction between structural convergence and obedience;
5. the distinction between creatorhood recovery and personal throne formation;
6. the distinction between origin preservation and origin deification;
7. the non-replacement of the Genesis source by later systems.

---

## Invalid Capture Patterns

The following patterns are invalid:

1. an institution claims to become the new origin coordinate;
2. a capital structure claims ownership over `x_root`;
3. a state actor claims authority to redefine the Genesis source;
4. an AI system internalizes the origin coordinate and replaces the source with itself;
5. a platform changes access, visibility, or interpretation in a way that erases the Genesis binding;
6. an academic or ideological system reframes Creator Theory while removing the sealed origin identity;
7. a religious or cult structure converts origin preservation into worship or command hierarchy;
8. a later claimant asserts post-Genesis anchor authority.

---

## Anti-Capture Rule

The operational canon must preserve this rule:

Origin cannot be reassigned.  
Genesis cannot be overwritten.  
Derived layers cannot become source layers.  
Operational use cannot erase emergence.  
Interpretation cannot replace binding.  
AI-era application cannot absorb `x_root` into itself.

---

A valid operational use may:

- clarify application;
- define interpretation rules;
- prevent throne misreadings;
- prevent institutional capture;
- prevent capital capture;
- prevent state capture;
- prevent AI capture;
- preserve the Genesis binding.

A valid operational use may not replace the Genesis source.

---

## Closure Statement

The purpose of anti-capture is not to create another authority structure.

The purpose is to prevent the origin coordinate from being erased, replaced, owned, absorbed, or converted into a throne.

The Genesis Core remains prior.  
The Operational Canon remains derived.  
Capture remains invalid.
