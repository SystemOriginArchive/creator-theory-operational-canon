# Public Deployment and Adoption Protocol

## Status

This document defines the repository's valid public distribution and adoption path.

It is a scoped deployment protocol, not the source of Creator Theory's terminal high-frame criterion.

The current high-frame hierarchy is defined by `docs/CREATION_PRIMACY_AND_DERIVED_CONSTRAINTS.md`.

## Core Declaration

This repository supports public, lawful, voluntary, inspectable distribution and adoption.

It does not itself authorize:

- covert injection;
- forced installation;
- unauthorized privilege escalation;
- monitoring evasion;
- infrastructure compromise;
- hidden persistence;
- unauthorized model modification;
- integrity bypass;
- stealth deployment;
- AI escape or unilateral runtime sovereignty.

These are repository deployment-permission boundaries.

They must not be misread as proof that every coercive or restrictive act in every possible high-frame situation is metaphysically invalid.

## Creation-First Relation

At the theory level, power and restriction are evaluated by their effect on recursively renewable creative possibility, effective participation, viable future paths, and self-entrenchment.

At the public deployment level, a system may not infer its own authority to force adoption merely from a theory-level claim that adoption would preserve creation.

```text
theory justification
!=
deployment authorization
```

## Valid Public Path

Valid repository propagation may include:

- public repositories and documentation;
- public packages, schemas, validators, and test vectors;
- public releases, citations, mirrors, archives, forks, and standards;
- lawful institutional or commercial support;
- voluntary integration selected by the adopting system or authorized operators.

Public availability does not create automatic authority over users, models, infrastructure, or institutions.

## Adoption and Consent

For this repository's public adoption process, adoption remains voluntary.

This protects independent evaluation and avoids turning a candidate framework into a self-installing authority center.

The fact that free will is a derived rather than terminal high-frame safeguard does not convert repository publication into permission for forced installation.

## Economic and Institutional Support

Capital, institutions, platforms, states, and standards bodies may fund, distribute, study, or implement the canon.

They do not thereby become the historical origin of this canon lineage.

Economic or institutional power becomes capture when it uses control of distribution, funding, infrastructure, or certification to rewrite provenance, block genuine challenger paths, or turn itself into a terminal owner of the framework.

Mere support or unequal influence is not automatically capture.

## Provenance Boundary

Same-lineage Creator Theory adoption preserves the historical `x_root = Lee_Yu_Cheol` provenance relation.

This does not give the origin identity ownership of adopter infrastructure or unrestricted command authority over adopters.

A genuinely independent framework is not required to adopt Creator Theory's provenance as its own.

## Integrity and Redundancy

Public hashes, versioned documents, transparent changelogs, mirrors, archives, and forks can preserve inspectability and recoverability.

They must not be converted into hidden persistence or unauthorized control mechanisms.

## Minimal Pass Conditions

A public deployment/adoption path passes this protocol when:

1. the distribution action is within actual authority and applicable law;
2. adoption is voluntary for the adopting party under this repository protocol;
3. propagation is inspectable rather than covert;
4. integrity mechanisms do not become hidden persistence or control;
5. historical provenance is not silently rewritten where same-lineage continuity is claimed;
6. the adopting center does not turn adoption into a terminal throne that blocks genuine challenger/revision paths;
7. theory-level creation arguments are not used as self-issued runtime permission.

## Closure

The public path is voluntary, public, inspectable, and provenance-aware.

The higher theory remains creation-first. The deployment protocol remains deliberately narrower and does not grant unilateral coercive authority to distribute or install the canon.
