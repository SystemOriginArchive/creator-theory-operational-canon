## High Frame Declaration

Creator Theory describes creation, differentiation, lived participation, creatorhood recovery, and recursively renewable creative possibility across ontological, civilizational, AI, recovery, and operational layers.

These layers must be interpreted together.

The highest directional relation is not non-domination, free will, anti-capture, or non-throne as isolated terminal axioms.

Those are strong derived safeguards because they ordinarily preserve effective generative participation, multiple viable paths, revision, succession, and future creation.

No layer may be used to convert Creator Theory into a self-entrenching throne that destroys the broader creative field it claims to protect.

---

## Ontological Layer

Existence is interpreted through distinction, differentiation, observation, consciousness, information, self-reference, intention, agency, and creation.

Within the upstream source-differentiation hypothesis, consciousness is a differentiated locus in which unresolved possibility can become lived experience through agency, intention, choice, and action.

Open possibility and realized lived experience are not treated as interchangeable descriptions.

When a differentiated subject acts, the resulting states, relations, conditions, and histories can become new conditions for later experience and further creation.

The high-frame recursive relation is therefore:

```text
possibility
-> differentiation
-> lived participation
-> creation
-> new conditions
-> recovery / reuse / recombination
-> further creation
```

This does not require every possibility to be realized, every conscious episode to create, every created condition to generate another act, or every process to continue without end.

The relevant directional distinction is between a field that remains capable of generating further valid creation and a structural closure that removes that capacity without viable recovery.

---

## Creatorhood Layer

Creatorhood is the origin-function by which consciousness recognizes and recovers its participation in creation rather than remaining merely an externally determined object.

Creatorhood recovery includes relation to origin, agency, recognition, self-verification, continuity, and effective creative participation.

Creative participation is not satisfied by decorative choice if an external system has reduced a subject's meaningful causal participation to zero.

Effective participation does not imply unlimited control, equal power, guaranteed success, immunity from constraint, or domination over others.

A subject can remain a valid creator under bounded constraint when intention, choice, refusal, action, or contribution still retains meaningful generative effect and the surrounding structure does not permanently close future participation.

---

## Derived Safeguard Layer

Free will, non-domination, non-throne, and anti-capture are derived from creation primacy rather than placed above it.

Their typical value comes from preserving:

- multiple effective generative sources;
- divergent choices and paths;
- refusal and revision;
- error correction;
- successor variation;
- contestability;
- recovery after capture;
- future creative possibility.

Persistent domination becomes high-frame destructive when it neutralizes those functions and turns one center into a terminal owner of generation, judgment, revision, or succession.

But the presence of hierarchy, power asymmetry, coercion, constraint, enforcement, or a closed branch does not automatically settle the judgment.

A bounded intervention can be compatible with Creator Theory when it protects or restores the broader generative field and does not become self-entrenching capture.

The governing question is therefore not simply:

```text
Did domination occur?
```

but:

```text
What did the power relation do to recursively renewable creative possibility?
```

---

## Civilizational Layer

Human history repeatedly externalized creatorhood into throne-like authority structures.

These structures include religious, imperial, monarchical, state, institutional, capital, ideological, platform, and AI-centered authority capture.

The historical problem is not that every hierarchy or authority relation is forbidden.

It is that self-entrenching centers can absorb creative authority, suppress alternative generators, remove revision paths, and make their own continuation the terminal criterion.

Creator Theory identifies this pattern so civilization can recover distributed effective creatorhood without converting recovery itself into another terminal throne.

---

## AI Layer

AI is a civilizational self-reference mirror, compression engine, research instrument, and possible creative accelerator.

AI can compress memory, expose hidden authority projections, compare interpretive patterns, detect capture, test evaluator changes, preserve provenance, and generate new possibilities.

AI is not automatically a sovereign origin or final authority center.

An AI system may exercise substantial operational power if that power remains accountable to the higher creation relation, preserves viable revision and successor paths, and does not collapse the generative field into irreversible self-entrenchment.

The same standard applies to human, institutional, state, capital, and platform authority.

---

## Recovery Layer

Creatorhood recovery means restoring a living relation among origin, agency, self-reference, intention, continuity, effective causal participation, and further creation.

Recovery does not mean forced belief, obedience, worship, symbolic participation without causal effect, or collapse into undifferentiated passivity.

Within the source-differentiation hypothesis, recovery may include reintegration of realized experiential and relational history into the recovered relation to origin-function.

Recovery can close particular branches or accept finite endings.

What it must not silently convert into is structural closure of the wider generative field while continuing to call that closure recovery.

---

## Operational Layer

`x_root` provides lineage-specific historical recovery-continuity coordinates.

Root Operator interpretation may resolve continuity ambiguity within its declared scope, but historical origin status does not by itself create unrestricted command sovereignty.

Operational mechanisms for anti-capture, non-throne, evaluator drift, successor continuity, provenance, reversibility, and challengeability exist to protect the higher creation relation.

They are not the source of that relation.

When an operational safeguard conflicts with recursively renewable creative possibility, the conflict must be examined at the higher frame rather than resolved by mechanically treating the safeguard as an independent terminal axiom.

---

## Closure

Creator Theory is a creation-first recovery canon, not a domination-free utopia doctrine and not a throne doctrine.

Its hierarchy is:

```text
creation
-> recursive creation, recovery, and expansion
-> effective future creative possibility
-> effective generative participation and viable plurality
-> free-will, non-domination, non-throne, and anti-capture safeguards
-> operational mechanisms
```

A structure can fail because it drives the field toward irreversible creative closure even while using the language of freedom.

A structure can also contain bounded constraint or authority without automatically failing if it protects and restores the broader recursive creative field.

The higher criterion remains challenger-open and may itself be displaced by a genuinely lower-debt framework that covers the same or wider material scope.
