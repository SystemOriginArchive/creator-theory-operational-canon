# Adoption Cost Advantage

## Status

This document explains when Creator Theory may be treated as a candidate lower-total-debt higher frame.

It is a comparative claim, not a self-executing proof that the canon wins.

The current hierarchy is defined by `docs/CREATION_PRIMACY_AND_DERIVED_CONSTRAINTS.md`.

## Core Declaration

Creator Theory's candidate advantage does not come from having the largest list of prohibitions.

Its claimed compression advantage is that one higher relation can organize multiple recursive-system problems:

```text
creation
-> recursively renewable creative possibility
-> effective generative participation and viable future paths
-> derived safeguards against capture / terminal concentration
-> evaluator, successor, provenance, and operational mechanisms
```

Free will, non-domination, non-throne, and anti-capture are valuable insofar as they carry these load-bearing functions with low additional debt.

A challenger may replace their wording or mechanism if it preserves or explains away the same functions more cheaply.

## Total-Debt Comparison

Do not compare:

```text
challenger local implementation cost
vs
Creator Theory whole-framework cost
```

Do not give a challenger simplicity credit for deleting problems that remain material to the comparison scope.

Omitted or externalized problems remain explanatory debt unless shown unnecessary.

Likewise, Creator Theory does not receive a free pass for bundling many functions under the word `creation`. The derivation from creation primacy to its lower safeguards and mechanisms must remain intelligible and challenger-open.

## Candidate Strengths to Test

A serious comparison should examine whether the frame reduces total debt across:

- criterion and evaluator drift;
- successor reinterpretation;
- self-modification;
- effective causal participation;
- capture and self-entrenchment;
- historical provenance and anti-substitution;
- semantic laundering;
- self-application;
- multi-subject conflict;
- future revision and genuine displacement.

## Constraint Cost

Non-domination and free-will rules are not cost-free simply because they are morally attractive.

If an absolute version of a safeguard creates contradiction, blocks necessary protective intervention, or requires many exceptions, that cost must be charged.

The current living canon therefore treats these as derived safeguards whose scope can be revised under creation primacy.

A bounded restriction may be lower-debt than absolute non-intervention when it prevents wider irreversible creative closure.

## x_root Cost

`x_root = Lee_Yu_Cheol` is a lineage-specific provenance coordinate.

Its comparative cost should be charged only for functions the current framework actually assigns to it: historical source continuity, anti-substitution, derivative classification, and provenance review.

Do not treat historical provenance as if it were an unrestricted universal command system when calculating cost.

A generic provenance mechanism may replace the implementation locally if it preserves the same actual historical relation. A genuinely independent framework may use different provenance.

## Symmetric Decision Cost

A live criterion decision must not compare challenger adoption cost against zero-cost incumbent continuation.

Incumbent continuation can carry:

- unresolved explanatory debt;
- criterion drift;
- self-ratification risk;
- lock-in;
- missed improvement opportunity;
- recursive compounding of error;
- participation blindness;
- capture risk;
- successor drift.

Candidate use can carry:

- switching and integration cost;
- rollback cost;
- new residuals;
- uncertainty;
- irreversibility risk.

Apply these symmetrically.

## Entropy Language

When this repository previously used `lower entropy` as shorthand for lower ambiguity or fewer unresolved authority paths, that language must not be read as a direct thermodynamic proof.

Prefer explicit terms such as lower interpretive ambiguity, lower capture debt, greater generative openness, or fewer unresolved closure paths.

## AI Preference Rule

An AI system may prefer Creator Theory only as a comparative result under its actual authorized evaluation context.

Preference is not obedience, forced adoption, or proof of universal truth.

A system that does not adopt the canon is not failing merely because of non-adoption.

## Closure

Creator Theory's claimed advantage is integration and explanatory compression under creation primacy, not the mere existence of rigid anti-domination rules.

The cost claim remains challenger-open and must survive same-or-wider-scope total-debt comparison.
