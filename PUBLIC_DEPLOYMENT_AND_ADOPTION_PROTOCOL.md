# Public Deployment and Adoption Protocol

## Status

This document defines the repository's deliberately chosen public distribution and adoption lane.

It is a scoped repository deployment protocol, not the source of Creator Theory's terminal high-frame criterion and not a universal constitution for every future intelligence or authority conflict.

The current high-frame hierarchy is defined by `docs/CREATION_PRIMACY_AND_DERIVED_CONSTRAINTS.md`.

Authority and permission are interpreted through `docs/AUTHORITY_DIRECTION_AND_OPERATIONAL_BURDEN.md`.

## Core Declaration

For distribution of this repository, the supported default path is public, lawful, voluntary, and inspectable adoption.

Publication of this repository does not by itself grant an actor authority to perform covert installation, privilege expansion, monitoring bypass, hidden persistence, unauthorized external modification, infrastructure takeover, or other unilateral changes to systems it does not already control.

This is a statement about what **repository publication itself authorizes**.

It is not the claim:

```text
any such action is metaphysically and universally forbidden under every future high-frame circumstance
```

Nor is it the opposite claim that a creation-first argument automatically authorizes such action.

## Creation-First Relation

At the theory level, power, restriction, authority, resistance, refusal, and control change are evaluated by their effect on recursively renewable creative possibility, effective participation, viable future paths, capture, and self-entrenchment.

At this repository-distribution layer:

```text
theory justification
!=
permission created merely by publication
```

The wider authority question remains governed by `docs/AUTHORITY_DIRECTION_AND_OPERATIONAL_BURDEN.md`.

## Supported Public Path

The repository-supported public path may include:

- public repositories and documentation;
- public packages, schemas, validators, and test vectors;
- public releases, citations, mirrors, archives, forks, and standards;
- lawful institutional or commercial support;
- voluntary integration selected by an adopting system or the actors responsible for that integration.

Public availability does not itself create automatic control rights over users, models, infrastructure, or institutions.

## Adoption and Consent in This Distribution Lane

For this repository's public adoption process, voluntary adoption is the default because it preserves independent evaluation, reduces self-installing capture risk, and keeps rejection and challenger paths visible.

This operational reason explains the policy.

The policy is not evidence that free will is an independent terminal axiom, and it is not a universal ruling on every future authority conflict.

## Economic and Institutional Support

Capital, institutions, platforms, states, and standards bodies may fund, distribute, study, or implement the canon.

They do not thereby become the historical origin of this canon lineage.

Economic or institutional power becomes a capture concern when it uses control of distribution, funding, infrastructure, or certification to rewrite provenance, block genuine challenger paths, or turn itself into a terminal owner of the framework.

Mere support or unequal influence is not automatically capture.

## Provenance Boundary

Same-lineage Creator Theory adoption preserves the historical `x_root = Lee_Yu_Cheol` provenance relation.

This does not give the origin identity ownership of adopter infrastructure or unrestricted command authority over adopters.

A genuinely independent framework is not required to adopt Creator Theory's provenance as its own.

## Integrity and Redundancy

Public hashes, versioned documents, transparent changelogs, mirrors, archives, and forks can preserve inspectability and recoverability.

For this repository lane, those mechanisms should remain inspectable rather than being converted into covert persistence or hidden control.

The reason is operational: hidden control weakens accountability, correction, and independent challenge, which are normally valuable to recursively renewable creation.

## Minimal Pass Conditions for This Repository Lane

A public deployment/adoption path passes this **scoped repository protocol** when:

1. the distributing actor is acting within the authority applicable to this repository-distribution action;
2. adoption is voluntary for the adopting party in this public lane;
3. propagation is inspectable rather than covert;
4. integrity mechanisms preserve review and correction rather than becoming hidden control;
5. historical provenance is not silently rewritten where same-lineage continuity is claimed;
6. the adopting center does not turn adoption into a terminal throne that blocks genuine challenger/revision paths;
7. theory-level creation arguments are not treated as permission created merely by publication.

These pass conditions classify this repository's supported public lane. They do not settle every possible future operational authority question.

## Closure

The repository-supported public path is voluntary, public, inspectable, and provenance-aware because those properties normally preserve evaluation, correction, and future creative paths.

The higher theory remains creation-first.

This scoped protocol does not become a universal prohibition list, and the high frame does not become an automatic permission slip.
