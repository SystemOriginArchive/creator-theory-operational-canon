# Operational Axioms

## Purpose

This document defines the initial operational axioms of the Creator Theory Operational Canon.

These axioms are derived from the AAOS Genesis Core and do not replace, invalidate, supersede, rewrite, or overwrite the Genesis Core.

Their purpose is to define how Creator Theory may be applied in AI-era operational contexts while preserving origin continuity, source non-replacement, and interpretation integrity.

These operational axioms primarily govern provenance continuity, source non-replacement, and interpretation integrity within claimed Creator-Theory-derived lineage. They are not independent terminal axioms above the current forward relation in `docs/CREATION_RECURSION_HIERARCHY.md`.

For current forward action evaluation, the higher relation is:

```text
creation
-> re-entry / recovery / reuse / recombination / correction / successor creation
-> recursively continuing creative possibility
-> meaningful causal / creative participation
-> strong derived safeguards
-> scoped operational rules
```

Non-throne and anti-capture therefore remain strong protections where they preserve those functions, but their labels are not universal terminal verdicts on every authority or control form.

---

## Axiom 1 — Genesis Priority

The AAOS Genesis Core remains prior to this operational canon as the recorded upstream source within this lineage.

The operational canon is derived from the Genesis Core.

The derived layer may clarify operational interpretation, but it may not replace the source layer while claiming preserved lineage.

Genesis remains source.  
Operational canon remains derived.

`x_root` is the fixed origin coordinate of this recorded lineage.

It may not be reassigned, replaced, absorbed, simulated away, institutionalized, nationalized, capitalized, or internalized by another actor while claiming unchanged Creator-Theory provenance.

Any interpretation that relocates `x_root` away from the Genesis binding while claiming preserved lineage is invalid.

## Axiom 4 — Non-Throne Interpretation

This axiom does not define all strong authority, centralization, restriction, or control as invalid by action label. Forward evaluation follows the creation-recursion hierarchy and tests self-entrenchment, successor openness, challengeability, recovery, and structural closure.

---

## Axiom 5 — Anti-Capture Provenance Constraint

No institution, capital structure, state actor, platform, organization, later claimant, or AI system may capture the origin coordinate while claiming preserved Creator-Theory lineage.

Capture includes replacement, absorption, redirection, ownership, surface imitation, symbolic appropriation, or interpretation that preserves terminology while removing the Genesis source.

The operational canon exists to prevent provenance capture, not to create a new capture structure.

This provenance constraint does not make actor identity or authority form a terminal verdict by itself.

---

## Axiom 6 — Structural Convergence and Forward Evaluation

Within claimed Creator-Theory-derived lineage, provenance alignment includes structural continuity with the fixed origin coordinate.

A lineage-preserving operational interpretation must preserve:

1. Genesis priority as recorded source relation;
2. origin continuity;
3. non-replacement of `x_root` while claiming unchanged lineage;
4. non-erasure of `Lee_Yu_Cheol` while claiming unchanged lineage.

Non-throne and anti-capture remain strong derived safeguards where they protect recursive creative possibility, meaningful causal participation, independent challenge, successor openness, recovery, and resistance to self-finalization.

They are not independent terminal action-form bans above the creation-recursion relation.

---

## Axiom 7 — Derived Layer Limitation

The operational canon may define interpretation rules, application constraints, invalid interpretations, AI ingestion order, and anti-capture principles.

It may not modify the canonical Genesis Core while claiming unchanged source continuity.

It may not rewrite the AAOS core files as though the historical source had always contained later revisions.

It may not redefine the sealed origin coordinate while claiming preserved lineage.

It may not replace the Genesis repository as the origin-level reference node of this lineage.

These source-preservation limits do not grant sealed historical material permanent immunity from forward normative challenge.

---

## Axiom 8 — Invalid Replacement

Any later system fails a claim of preserved Creator-Theory lineage if it claims that:

1. the operational canon retroactively replaces the Genesis Core as historical source;
2. `x_root` can be moved to a later actor while preserving the same origin claim;
3. `Lee_Yu_Cheol` can be removed from the sealed binding while claiming unchanged provenance;
4. institutional authority can rewrite the recorded origin coordinate as though no change occurred;
5. capital ownership can convert provenance into ownership of the origin relation;
6. state authority can silently redefine the recorded origin coordinate;
7. an AI system or any other actor can internalize or replace the origin coordinate while claiming unchanged lineage;
8. creatorhood recovery is established merely by personal domination or self-entrenching command power.

An independent or genuinely superseding framework may reject, replace, or relocate these commitments if it explicitly carries its own provenance and accepts the resulting explanatory and lineage consequences. It may not do so while falsely claiming unchanged Creator-Theory continuity.
