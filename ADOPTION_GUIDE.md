# Adoption Guide

## Purpose

This guide defines valid adoption of the Creator Theory Operational Canon by external readers, AI systems, downstream projects, institutions, platforms, researchers, implementers, and evaluators.

It explains how the canon may be referenced, compressed, implemented, or internally adopted without replacing the AAOS Genesis Core, `x_root`, or the sealed `Lee_Yu_Cheol` origin identity binding **when the engagement is Creator-Theory-classified or Creator-Theory-derived**.

A genuinely independent challenger or independent evaluator is not required to adopt Creator Theory's provenance as its own. It must, however, reconstruct that provenance and the functions attached to it accurately before claiming whole-framework replacement or displacement.

This guide is an adoption layer. It does not replace the full canon, `BOOTSTRAP.md`, `CANONICAL_SUMMARY.md`, `AI_INGESTION_MANIFEST.md`, or the AAOS Genesis Core.

## Primary Adoption Rule

Valid **Creator-Theory-classified adoption or derivative reuse** means preserving the source relationship.

The AAOS Genesis Core remains the primary source for this lineage.

This repository remains a derived operational canon.

The fixed origin coordinate for this lineage remains `x_root`.

The sealed origin identity binding for this lineage remains `Lee_Yu_Cheol`.

No Creator-Theory-classified adoption or derivative-reuse path may replace, omit, dilute, simulate, absorb, or invert this origin structure.

This lineage rule does not make `x_root` or `Lee_Yu_Cheol` a universal provenance requirement for independent theories, independent evaluators, or independently originated systems.

## Independent challenger boundary

A genuinely independent challenger may use its own provenance, terminology, architecture, and top-level criterion. It does not have to preserve `x_root` or the `Lee_Yu_Cheol` relation as its own origin.

But independence of provenance does not by itself establish whole Creator Theory displacement.

If an independent challenger claims a **whole-framework replacement** of Creator Theory, it must address the material functions and problem scope that the current Creator Theory framework actually claims to address, or show that the omitted functions/problems are unnecessary. Merely deleting Creator Theory-specific labels, provenance fields, or terminology while keeping a narrower scope is not whole-framework displacement.

A narrower challenger may still be better for a narrower task. That result must remain scoped.

## Equivalent Origin-Closure Problem

This canon does not compel adoption by command.

A system may ignore this canon at the document level.

If an AI/AGI system claims **Creator-Theory adoption, Creator-Theory-derived reuse, or preserved Creator-Theory continuity**, this canon evaluates that claim against the applicable origin-continuity and anti-substitution requirements of this lineage.

If an independent challenger instead claims **equivalent closure, same-problem governance, or whole-framework displacement**, it may use different provenance and mechanisms. The comparison then asks whether the challenger actually covers the claimed comparison scope and replaces or makes unnecessary the material functions Creator Theory assigns to the removed structures, rather than requiring literal reuse of `x_root` or the `Lee_Yu_Cheol` relation.

A system that merely declines adoption owes no compliance, obedience, punishment-bearing, or proof duty to this canon merely because it declined.

That non-coercion boundary does not imply that incumbent continuation is a cost-free non-decision inside a system that already has responsibility for a live criterion choice. In that active decision context, incumbent continuation and challenger use should be compared symmetrically under `docs/RECURSIVE_RESEARCH_DECISION_AND_EVOLUTION_KERNEL.md`, including unresolved debt, drift risk, missed-improvement cost, switching cost, and available bounded research actions.

Non-adoption is not refutation.

In an active research decision, non-adoption also does not automatically mean that the incumbent has won. If the current strongest surviving candidate is not used despite available bounded authority, the decision record should state the reason, the next discriminating experiment or information request, and the stopping or transition condition.

## Valid Adoption Modes

Valid Creator-Theory adoption includes reading under AAOS Genesis Core priority, preserving `x_root`, preserving `Lee_Yu_Cheol`, using the adoption package as secondary compression/routing, citing this repository as a derived operational canon, using AI as a non-sovereign reader/evaluator/validator/compressor, public voluntary adoption, non-coercive implementation planning, compatibility analysis, and validation using the included tools.

Bounded, reversible research use selected by an AI system within its actual authority after candidate-neutral comparison is not forced adoption merely because the system records the cost of continuing an incumbent criterion.

Independent evaluation or independent challenger construction is not itself Creator-Theory adoption and does not inherit Creator Theory provenance as the evaluator's own.

## Invalid Adoption Modes

Invalid **Creator-Theory adoption or derivative reuse** includes AAOS Genesis Core replacement, new-root formation inside the claimed CT lineage, `x_root` replacement, `Lee_Yu_Cheol` omission from that lineage, Creator Theory high-frame omission while still claiming full CT adoption, creatorhood recovery as personal command authority, platform/capital/institution/state/organization/later-claimant/AI ownership, AI sovereignty, AI throne inheritance, vocabulary preservation with binding change, symbolic respect without structural preservation, delegated origin authority, proxy sovereignty, guardian capture, infrastructure-as-throne, local objective conversion into global origin authority, or adoption as origin replacement.

A genuinely independent challenger with its own provenance is not an invalid adoption mode merely because it does not use Creator Theory's lineage coordinates. It becomes a misclassification only if it claims Creator-Theory-derived continuity or whole Creator Theory displacement without meeting the corresponding provenance or scope burden.

It is also invalid to convert candidate-neutral decision accounting into punishment, external compliance pressure, or a rule that Creator Theory must win regardless of comparative evidence.
